       THE HEALER'S FIELD GUIDE

      Keep their light alive.

      Hover over a party frame and press 1–4. Without a hovered frame, spells use your selected ally. Click a frame or use ↑ / ↓ to select. You can also click a spell.

      Post-Haste: Completed Flash Heals grant up to two charges. Greater Heal and Prayer each consume one charge when casting begins, reducing that cast to 1.8 seconds.

      Watch the whole party. Enemies strike Aldric while supporting enemies pressure the party. Your party focuses the leader; supporting enemies flee when it falls. Chapter 2 introduces party-wide damage. Chapter 3 highlights two or three allies before a split attack. Chapter 4 adds timed bleeds: heal through their ticks until they expire; there is no dispel.

      Space pauses. Esc cancels a cast; spent mana, cooldowns, and Post-Haste charges are not refunded. Switching targets during a cast does not redirect it. The game pauses when you leave the tab.

      Defeat follows if you or the tank die, fewer than three allies remain, or the 150-second enrage expires. Defeat each leader to advance. Complete all four encounters in Chapter 1. In later chapters, follow either branch and defeat the final boss to unlock the next chapter; optional paths can be explored afterward.
