## Active-healing audit and tuning

Across 13,536 full-resource idle/one-HoT/one-Wave/one-Stream probes, there are **0 safe clears**. The baseline had 46, concentrated at Chapter 5's farming entrance, plus Chapter 6's entrance. With the final Reedstalker correction, all sampled intended-gear fights require more than one spell. The full-route browser pass showed frequent triage and active casting. Four-boss pressure is now persistent through the phase windows; average-gear boss durations rise 5: 129s, 6: 143s, 7: 154s, 8: 174s.

Changes are confined to Chapter 5–8 encounter HP, strike and authored mechanic damage in `src/data.js`. Opening Chapter 5–6 strikes prevent effectively idle farming without blocking inherited gear. Later normals gain stronger split, bleed and group overlaps. Elites increase risk for their targeted reward chance. Boss HP and phase damage prolong meaningful pressure; Chapter 8 remains the longest and has four distinct states. No healer values, global Mana values, Chapter 1–4 encounters or gear, or BAT-98 loot/reward rules changed.
